METANOIA

A visual diary of night, people, places and the moments that make ordinary life feel a little less ordinary.
01 — The idea

Look again.
Feel more.

Metanoia is about the moments you might normally walk past: a platform waiting for the last train, light cutting through a club, a stranger caught mid-laugh, colour appearing where you least expect it.

02 — Selected work

Fragments of life.

03 — About

Not everything
needs a reason.

Metanoia is a personal photography project built around curiosity — the instinct to stop, look closer, and keep the frame.

Some images are quiet. Some are loud. Some are deliberately imperfect. Together they form a visual diary of cities, nightlife, people, colour, and the small details that stay with you long after the moment is gone.
